Our story

Built on the Land We Love

Evergreen Land Solutions has been helping Hill Country property owners build smarter, more beautiful landscapes since 2011.

A Hill Country Company, Through and Through

Evergreen Land Solutions was founded in 2011 by a team of irrigation specialists who grew up in the Texas Hill Country and understood its unique challenges — thin soils, rocky terrain, unpredictable rainfall, and the constant pressure to conserve water.

What started as a one-truck irrigation service has grown into a full-service landscaping and irrigation company that services Kerrville, Fredericksburg, Boerne, Marble Falls, New Braunfels, and beyond.

We're not a national franchise. Every project is managed locally, every crew member is trained in-house, and every system we install is designed specifically for the property it serves. That's the Evergreen difference.
